Executive snapshot

The product in one minute.

Payouts is a supplemental compensation distribution capability—not a payroll engine and not a payment-recovery product.

Commercial modelFortuna contracts directly with Employer

MSA governs the relationship; Payouts Agreement governs product-specific obligations.

Payment typeNet compensation only

Tips, bonuses, commissions, incentives, approved reimbursements and off-cycle payments.

FundingACH / Wire

Employers prefund before Payouts can be distributed.

DistributionInstant ACH

To eligible All-In-One Accounts or Paycards; timing is not guaranteed.

Reserve floorBalance must remain > $1,000

If projected balance is $1,000 or less, the batch is paused.

Maximum balance$100,000 standard

Unless otherwise approved or changed for banking, regulatory, compliance, or risk requirements.

AccuracyEmployer responsibility

The platform cannot determine whether the compensation amount itself is correct.

FinalityNo reversal in V1

Submitted Payouts are one-way and do not include recovery or correction tooling.

Product problem

Supplemental pay needs a controlled distribution path.

Employers need a simple way to move compensation outside their normal payroll cycle without making Fortuna responsible for compensation calculations or tax treatment.

Operating model

Three parties. Three clear responsibilities.

The V1 model intentionally separates compensation decisions, program administration, and technology execution.

Employer

Determines who is paid and how much. Owns payroll, taxes, eligibility, authorization, and business accuracy.

Fortuna

Owns the Employer relationship, onboarding, program administration, funding coordination, and operational support.

Grit

Provides the underlying technology and executes valid Payment Instructions within the agreed product scope.

End-to-end journey

From prefunding to employee receipt.

The bulk workflow lets the Employer prepare Net Payment Amounts offline while retaining in-product validation and final authorization.

Template instruction: Select one or more eligible employees before downloading the Excel template. The template includes only the employees selected. After entering net payout amounts, upload the completed template for validation before submitting the Payout.

Core business rules

Funding controls prevent unsupported Payouts.

V1 relies on prefunding, reserve controls, validation, and explicit Employer authorization as primary safeguards.

> $1K

Available Balance must remain greater than $1,000.

If a Payout would result in a balance of exactly $1,000 or less, the entire batch is paused. Employer must fund the account, reduce payment amounts, or remove recipients.

Business ruleV1 behavior
Low-balance thresholdAt $1,000, the platform generates a courtesy alert to designated contacts. Failure to receive an alert does not remove the Employer's funding responsibility.
Maximum funding balance$100,000 unless otherwise approved by Fortuna or modified for applicable banking, regulatory, compliance, or risk requirements.
Net amounts onlyPayouts does not calculate gross pay, taxes, withholding, garnishments, deductions, benefits, or payroll filings.
Employer authorizationSubmission represents that the payment is authorized, the recipient and Net Payment Amount are correct, payroll/tax calculations are complete, the payment is lawful, and funds are sufficient.
Instant ACHDescribes the intended distribution method. Immediate settlement or availability is not guaranteed.
Example: Available Balance = $2,350. Proposed Payout = $1,500. Projected Balance = $850. Because $850 is below the operating reserve, the batch cannot proceed.

Validation

Technical validation is not compensation validation.

This distinction is central to the V1 product and legal model.

Payouts can validate

  • Required fields and file structure
  • Employee eligibility
  • Payment format
  • Available Balance
  • Operating reserve
  • Applicable limits
  • Certain duplicate conditions
  • Other supported system rules

Payouts cannot determine

  • Whether the selected employee is the intended employee
  • Whether $100 or $1,000 is the correct amount
  • Whether a tip or commission calculation is correct
  • Whether payroll calculations are correct
  • Whether compensation is legitimately owed beyond Employer authorization

Payment finality

One-way by design.

V1 intentionally prioritizes prevention over remediation because it does not contain a payment-correction engine.

→

Employer → Employee

After final submission and entry into processing, V1 does not provide reversal, recall, employee debit, overpayment recovery, recipient change, Employer reimbursement, payroll offset, or automated correction functionality.

Recommended final confirmation:
Confirm that each employee and Net Payment Amount is correct. Once submitted for processing, payments are final and cannot be reversed, adjusted, recovered, or corrected through Payouts.

Exception model

Failed is different from erroneous.

Operations and reporting must distinguish a processing failure from a correctly executed but incorrect Employer instruction.

Failed Payout

The Employer submitted the intended instruction, but it could not successfully complete.

  • Closed or ineligible destination
  • Invalid destination information
  • Bank/network rejection
  • Compliance or fraud review
  • Technical processing failure

Erroneous Payout

The system correctly executed an Employer instruction that was itself wrong.

  • Wrong employee
  • Wrong amount
  • Overpayment
  • Underpayment
Failed Payout handling: Where applicable, funds should return to Available Balance after failure is confirmed and the relevant settlement/return process completes. V1 should not promise a specific re-credit SLA, automatic retry, or employee notification unless those capabilities are operationally confirmed.

V1 scope

What ships—and what deliberately does not.

The scope line should remain consistent across the Employer experience, Fortuna operating procedures, the MSA, the Payouts Agreement, and the Grit–Fortuna product scope.

In scope

  • Direct Employer onboarding
  • Employer Payout enablement
  • Authorized User management
  • Eligible employee selection
  • XLSX template generation
  • Offline Net Payment Amount entry
  • Template upload
  • File and eligibility validation
  • Available Balance validation
  • $1,000 operating reserve
  • Funding controls
  • Review and confirmation
  • Employer authorization
  • Instant ACH distribution
  • All-In-One / Paycard destination
  • Transaction reporting
  • Failed Payout visibility
  • Auditability
  • Low-balance notification

Out of scope

  • Payroll processing
  • Tax calculation
  • Gross-to-net calculation
  • Determining appropriate compensation
  • Payment reversal
  • ACH recall through Admin Portal
  • Employee debit
  • Automated overpayment recovery
  • Automated payroll offset
  • Employer reimbursement
  • Employee repayment workflow
  • Automated payment correction
  • Payroll reconciliation
  • Employer / employee compensation dispute resolution
