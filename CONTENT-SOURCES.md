# Content sources and production review notes

## Primary source supplied for this project

**Overview Flyer - Grit for ABCorp, August 13, 2026**

The landing-page product claims, support details and legal framing were based primarily on the supplied flyer, including:

- Digital payroll through a Grit Visa Paycard
- Free registration and no monthly subscription fee, with other fees potentially applying
- Direct deposit and possible availability up to two days early, dependent on employer fund release
- 55,000+ Allpoint fee-free ATM access
- Physical and virtual card availability
- Mobile-wallet compatibility
- External bank transfers, including same-day for a fee and standard fee-free timing of 5-7 business days
- Physical-card delivery estimate of 5-7 business days
- MoneyGram availability for eligible customers after KYC, activation and final approval
- Grit support phone, email and service hours
- Community Federal Savings Bank, Visa and FDIC disclosures

## Items to validate before publishing

1. **Program choice and payroll authorization** - The page deliberately avoids saying the paycard is mandatory or optional. AB Corp HR/legal should add program-specific language if required.
2. **Employee ID** - It is optional in the current form. HR can make it required if needed for employee matching.
3. **Fees** - Confirm the current fee schedule linked from Grit Terms & Conditions before launch.
4. **Early pay** - Keep the dependency on employer release timing visible.
5. **MoneyGram** - Keep eligibility, KYC, approval, fees, exchange-rate, destination, limit and timing disclaimers visible.
6. **Identity-verification details** - The supplied flyer does not list the exact information or documents required. The FAQ therefore directs employees to Grit's secure enrollment flow or AB Corp HR rather than guessing at requirements.
7. **Vietnamese translation** - Have a native Vietnamese reviewer and compliance reviewer approve the final text.
8. **Google Sheet privacy** - Restrict access, set a retention policy and confirm AB Corp's privacy notice covers the contact request.
9. **Logo use** - Confirm internal approval for AB Corp, Visa, MoneyGram, CFSB, PCI and SOC logo display in the final campaign.

## Copy principle used

The page separates a low-risk HR contact request from the regulated account-opening process. It explicitly tells employees not to enter Social Security numbers, bank information, passwords or identity documents into the landing-page form.
